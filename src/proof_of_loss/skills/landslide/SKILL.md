# Landslide

A localized calamity under PMFBY: report within 72 hours.

Ask: when it happened, which part of the field is buried or washed away, crop and area.
Photos: the debris on the field from two angles, and the slope above it.
Weather data can support a rain-triggered landslide but cannot rule one out.
