# Post-harvest loss

PMFBY covers a crop kept "cut and spread" in the field to dry for up to 14 days after
harvest, against hailstorm, cyclonic rain and unseasonal rain. Report within 72 hours.

Ask:
1. On what date was the crop cut? If more than 14 days before the loss, tell the farmer
   gently that this cover may not apply, but still help them report.
2. Was the crop spread in the field to dry, or bundled and stacked? Stacked crops in a
   barn are not covered.
3. Date and time of the rain or hail; amount of harvested crop damaged (area in acres).

Photos: wet or sprouting grain, damaged bundles lying in the field.
Write the harvest date in the description passed to save_claim_details.
