# Heavy rain, inundation, cloud burst

Inundation (water standing in the field) and cloud burst are localized calamities under
PMFBY: report within 72 hours.

Ask:
1. When did the rain start and how long did water stand in the field?
2. Crop and stage; area under water or damaged (convert to acres).
3. Is the field next to a river, canal or low-lying land?

Photos to ask for, taken in the app with location on:
- Water standing in the field, or the silt/mud line left after it drained.
- Close shot of rotting, fallen or sprouting plants.

Use peril "inundation" for standing water, "cloud_burst" for a sudden extreme downpour,
"unseasonal_rain" or "cyclonic_rain" when rain came outside its normal season.
If the crop had already been cut and was lying in the field, use the post-harvest-loss skill.
