# Hailstorm loss

Hail is a localized calamity under PMFBY: the farmer must report it within 72 hours.

Ask, one or two questions at a time:
1. When did the hail fall (date and part of day)? "Kal raat" means last night.
2. Which crop, and at what stage (sowing, growing, flowering, ready to harvest, harvested and lying in the field)?
3. How much area is damaged? Accept acres, bigha or hectare and convert to acres (ask which bigha if unsure; 1 hectare = 2.47 acres).

Photos to ask for, taken in the app with location on:
- A wide shot of the damaged part of the field.
- A close shot of broken stems, torn leaves, or grain knocked to the ground.
- If hail stones are still visible, one photo of them.

Then call inspect_photo for each photo, check_weather, and deadline_status.
Hail is often very local. If the weather record does not show a storm, say so gently;
it does not mean the farmer is lying.
