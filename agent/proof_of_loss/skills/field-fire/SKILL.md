# Natural fire

PMFBY covers fire from natural causes (for example lightning). Fire started on purpose,
or from burning stubble nearby, is not a natural cause.

Ask: when the fire happened, how it started as far as the farmer knows, crop and area,
and whether the fire brigade or police were called (a report number helps a lot).
Photos: burnt area, edge between burnt and unburnt crop.
Weather data can show a thunderstorm but cannot prove or disprove a fire.
